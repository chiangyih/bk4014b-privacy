BK 4014B 控制與監看 App 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日

本隱私權政策適用於「新化高工資訊科 BK4014B 控制與監看」（以下稱「本 App」）。本 App 由 Tseng 發行，是用於 BK 4014B 訊號產生器之教學、模擬、狀態讀取與基本控制的 Windows 桌面應用程式。

我們處理的資料

本 App 不要求建立帳戶，也不收集姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人、相片、財務資料或其他使用者個人資料。

為提供本機功能，本 App 會在使用者的 Windows 裝置上處理可用及已選取的 COM 埠名稱、使用者輸入的波形參數，以及所連接儀器回覆的製造商、型號、韌體版本、可能的儀器序號、設定值、輸出狀態、工作模式、時間戳、命令回覆與錯誤資訊。

使用目的與儲存

上述技術資料僅用於列出序列埠、連接使用者選定的 BK 4014B、顯示或控制儀器狀態、執行模擬，以及在目前工作階段顯示操作與錯誤紀錄。

目前版本不會自動將這些資料寫入本機檔案、使用者設定檔、資料庫或雲端。工作階段資料保留於程式記憶體中，並在 App 關閉後不再由本 App 保留。

網路傳輸、分享與銷售

本 App 不會將上述資料傳送給發行者或第三方；不包含廣告、使用情況分析、追蹤或開發者遙測，也不出售或分享使用者資料。

Microsoft Store、Windows 或裝置驅動程式可能依其各自的條款與隱私權聲明處理下載、授權、當機或系統診斷資料；該等處理由相應服務提供者負責，並非本 App 傳送給發行者的資料。

裝置存取與使用者控制

本 App 會列出 Windows 偵測到的 COM 埠，只有在使用者選擇埠並按下連線後才與該序列埠通訊。使用者可隨時中斷連線或關閉 App，以停止本 App 對儀器資料的處理。因本 App 不將工作階段資料傳送或保存於發行者系統，發行者沒有可供查閱、下載或刪除的使用者資料副本。

兒童隱私

本 App 不要求帳戶，且不會刻意收集兒童或其他使用者的個人資料。

政策更新

若未來版本加入本機設定檔、匯入／匯出、線上服務、分析或其他會改變資料處理方式的功能，本政策將在該版本發佈前更新。最新版本會公布於本政策頁面。

Privacy Policy for the BK 4014B Controller and Monitor App

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to “新化高工資訊科 BK4014B 控制與監看” (the “App”). The App is published by Tseng and is a Windows desktop application for teaching, simulation, status readback, and basic control of a BK 4014B signal generator.

Data processed by the App

The App does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, financial information, or other user personal information.

To provide its local functions, the App processes on the user’s Windows device the names of available and selected COM ports, waveform parameters entered by the user, and technical information returned by the connected instrument. That technical information may include manufacturer, model, firmware version, instrument serial number if supplied, settings, output state, operating mode, timestamps, command responses, and error information.

Purpose and storage

The App uses this technical information only to list serial ports, connect to the user-selected BK 4014B, display or control the instrument, run the simulation, and show operation or error records during the current session.

The current version does not automatically save this information to local files, user profiles, databases, or cloud services. Session information is held in application memory and is no longer retained by the App after the App is closed.

Network transmission, sharing, and sale

The App does not transmit this information to the publisher or any third party. It contains no advertising, usage analytics, tracking, or publisher telemetry, and it does not sell or share user data.

Microsoft Store, Windows, or device drivers may process download, licensing, crash, or system diagnostic data under their own terms and privacy statements. Such processing is performed by the relevant service provider and is not data transmitted by the App to the publisher.

Device access and user controls

The App lists COM ports detected by Windows and communicates with a serial port only after the user selects the port and chooses to connect. The user may disconnect or close the App at any time to stop the App’s processing of instrument information. Because the App does not transmit or retain session information in publisher systems, the publisher has no copy of user data to access, download, or delete.

Children’s privacy

The App does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds local profiles, import/export, online services, analytics, or another feature that changes data handling, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The latest policy will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher, Tseng, through the public support page.
